How It Works

Read this first. It takes 60 seconds.

The Setup

Entry fee: $20 per entry. Unlimited entries allowed — submit as many lineups as you'd like.

Pick 5 teams from the 48 World Cup squads. Each team has a dollar price (see Team Prices). Your five picks must add up to $20 or less.

You can re-pick a team someone else picked — no exclusivity. Pick the teams you actually believe in.

Scoring

Across the whole tournament, for each of your five teams:

  • +5 points per goal scored by your team
  • −2 points per goal conceded by your team

Own goals count for the team they were scored for. Cumulative across group stage AND knockout rounds — teams that go deep are worth more.

The Payout

After all entries are collected, the total pot is split:

  • 70% to Wade & Steph (the wedding gift)
  • 30% to the top three entries:
    • 1st place: 60% of the prize pool
    • 2nd place: 30%
    • 3rd place: 10%

Ties

If multiple entries tie for a podium spot, the combined prize money for the tied positions gets split equally. Example: 10 entries tie for 1st → 1st + 2nd + 3rd prizes (100% of the prize pool) are added together and divided 10 ways. 3 entries tie for 2nd → 2nd + 3rd prizes are added and split evenly across those 3.

How to Enter

Go to Create Your Team → build your lineup → fill in your name and email → hit "Submit My Entry". You'll get a 5-digit confirmation code. Then send your $20 e-transfer to sctorres7@hotmail.com and put the confirmation code in the memo/message field so we can match the payment to your entry.

Want more than one entry? Submit again with a different team name (e.g. "Chaos Lineup", "Safe Bets"). Each entry is $20 and gets its own confirmation code.
